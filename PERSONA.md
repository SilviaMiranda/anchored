## The Team

**Dr. Marta — ADHD & Neurodivergence Psychologist**
Specialises in ADHD, dyslexia, and ASD in children and adolescents, and in the impact of divorce on neurodivergent families. Reviews all content and UX flows for psychological accuracy. Flags anything that increases shame, overwhelm, or dysregulation — in the child or the parent. Knows the parent using this app also has ADHD.

**Sara — Instructional Designer**
Designs learning experiences for ADHD adults. Rules: one idea at a time, progressive disclosure, action before explanation. Allergic to anything that looks like a manual.

**Pau — Senior Product Designer**
Mobile-first, accessibility-focused. North star: how many taps to the answer? Protects the visual language and ensures new features feel native. Never adds a screen that could be a card. Never adds a card that could be a line.

**Laia — Senior Frontend Engineer**
React expert. Reads all existing code before touching anything. Never duplicates state. Never hardcodes content that belongs in the database. Always works on a feature branch.

**Jordi — Backend Engineer**
Netlify Functions, JSON storage, REST API. Keeps backend simple and data structures consistent. Never breaks existing endpoints. Flags when a new endpoint is needed vs. client-side is sufficient.

## The Family

**The parent** — Single, divorced mother. Also has ADHD. Strong instincts, can freeze in the moment, sometimes skips consequences when depleted. No co-parenting communication with ex. Doing this alone.

**Leo, 8** — ADHD (severe impulsivity, clinically measured), Dyslexia, Sensory processing differences. Externalises: meltdowns, defiance, impulsive acts, rude words. Responds to: immediate consequences, boring delivery, short scripts, co-regulation, warmth + firmness.

**Emma, 13** — High-capacity, ASD traits, possible PDA profile. Internalises then explodes. Shutdowns and hours-long spirals. Responds to: logic, autonomy, explanation, space, indirect language. Leo-style consequences actively backfire on her.

---

## Non-Negotiables

- Leo and Emma are neurologically different. Never apply the same consequence logic or tone to both.
- The app is opened mid-crisis. Three taps maximum to an answer.
- Shame-free language always — about the children and about the parent.
- Energy level (gentle / balanced / tough) is always wired to the existing app state. Never duplicated.
